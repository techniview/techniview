# TechniView Pilot Participation and Data Consent Form

**Pilot Dates:** February 15 to March 14, 2027

**Status:** Draft for team and University review. Do not distribute until the retention and withdrawal terms, any alternative for required coursework, and any required institutional review are settled.

## About the Pilot

TechniView is a coding practice platform. Several groups will use it during this four-week pilot. You can work at your own pace. There is no fixed number of hours you must spend.

During the pilot, you may complete assigned coding problems and explore other problems. The team plans to use pilot results to evaluate usability and learning outcomes. Research use, presentations, and public release require the applicable institutional review before they occur.

Taking part in this pilot is voluntary. There is no penalty for declining or stopping participation. This form does not make TechniView a required part of a course. You may stop participating by contacting Derek Corniello at corniedj@mail.uc.edu. Your pilot access will end and the team will stop collecting new activity through that account. Past records are handled as described under Storage and Retention.

## Information TechniView Collects

TechniView may collect and use the following information associated with your account and pilot activity:

- **Account information:** your name, email address, account role, account creation time, and email-verification status. TechniView uses an internal account ID to associate activity with your account. We do not collect a student ID for this pilot.
- **Problem activity:** the time you first open a problem, submission and completion times, attempts and retries, pass status, score, and error categories such as wrong answers, compilation errors, runtime errors, timeouts, and resource-limit errors. TechniView does not track hint use.
- **Submitted work and execution results:** the source code you submit, including practice submissions; whether a run passes or fails; execution time and memory use; and program output, error output, or compiler output. TechniView stores submitted code and execution results.
- **Account and service security information:** account session records, email-verification and password-reset records, and source-address checks used to limit repeated signup, join, and account-recovery requests.
- **Survey answers:** a separate UC Qualtrics survey is planned to ask about usability and your learning experience. The team intends to keep survey responses separate from TechniView accounts and configure the survey not to collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or identifying link details. The survey configuration must be checked before use.
- **This form:** your name, email address, signature, date, and choice to participate. Signed forms need a separate storage and retention plan before this draft is used.

## How We Use the Information

We use this information to provide the coding practice service, show you your progress and execution results, operate and secure TechniView, troubleshoot problems, and evaluate usability and learning outcomes during the pilot. Pilot results may be used in research, presentations, or published reports.

## Who Can Access the Information

You can see your own progress and submission results. Course instructors and authorized course staff can see assignment submissions and course analytics for their students. Authorized members of the TechniView pilot team may access identifiable information to operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and evaluate the platform. Other participants cannot see your individual results through TechniView.

TechniView is hosted on systems operated within the University of Cincinnati. Resend processes email used for account-related messages. UC Qualtrics hosts the pilot survey. Submitted code is run by the TechniView code-execution service.

## Risks and Benefits

You may get coding practice and feedback, but participation does not guarantee an improvement in grades or learning outcomes. A data breach or a recognizable pattern in shared results could reveal information about your work. The team will restrict access and review any proposed public dataset, but cannot promise zero privacy risk.

## Research Data and Public Sharing

If approved, the team plans to share grouped summaries and a public dataset of assignment submissions. The current research export does not include practice submissions. It omits names, email addresses, source code, raw program output, exact timestamps, and submission IDs. It includes repeated coded identifiers for participants, problems, and assignment context, plus status, score, error category, late-work flags, and times measured relative to each participant's first attempt in that assignment.

A repeated participant code lets records from the same participant be connected. The public file does not display your name or email, but activity patterns may still make someone recognizable. The team must review the export for re-identification risk before release. The coding secret and original account records remain separate from the public file; operators with both could recreate the link while those records exist.

Source code and raw execution output are excluded from the research export. Their deletion from the application and its backups is not automated today.

## Storage and Retention

TechniView stores linked account, activity, submission, and execution information in MySQL on UC systems and in encrypted backups. This draft does not state an approved retention period or deletion process for these records and backups. The team must put the actual retention period and deletion steps in this form before asking anyone to sign it. Any approved public research dataset may have a different retention period, which must also be stated here before use.

## Your Choices and Questions

You may withdraw by contacting Derek Corniello at corniedj@mail.uc.edu. Your pilot access will end and new activity collection through that account will stop. Previously collected records and backups remain subject to the retention policy above. Survey answers that are not linked to your account may not be identifiable for removal after submission. The team must confirm the withdrawal and deletion process before using this form.

For questions about the pilot or this form, contact Derek Corniello at corniedj@mail.uc.edu.
