# TechniView Pilot Data Consent Form

**Pilot Dates:** February 15 to March 14, 2027

## About the Pilot

TechniView is a coding practice platform. Several groups will use it during this four-week pilot. You can work at your own pace. There is no fixed number of hours you must spend.

During the pilot, you may complete assigned coding problems and explore other problems. We will use pilot results to evaluate usability and learning outcomes and may use them in research, presentations, or published reports.

Taking part is voluntary. There is no penalty for declining or stopping participation. You may stop participating at any time by contacting Derek Corniello at corniedj@mail.uc.edu. If you withdraw, your access to the pilot will end and TechniView will stop collecting new activity about you. Existing data will be de-identified at the end of the pilot.

## Information TechniView Collects

TechniView may collect and use the following information associated with your account and pilot activity:

- **Account information:** your name, email address, account role, account creation time, and email-verification status. TechniView uses an internal account ID to associate activity with your account. We do not collect a student ID for this pilot.
- **Problem activity:** the time you first open a problem, submission and completion times, attempts and retries, pass status, score, and error categories such as wrong answers, compilation errors, runtime errors, timeouts, and resource-limit errors. TechniView does not track hint use.
- **Submitted work and execution results:** the source code you submit, including practice submissions; whether a run passes or fails; execution time and memory use; and program output, error output, or compiler output. TechniView stores submitted code and execution results.
- **Account and service security information:** account session records, email-verification and password-reset records, and source-address checks used to limit repeated signup, join, and account-recovery requests.
- **Survey answers:** a UC Qualtrics survey will ask about usability and your learning experience. Survey responses will not be linked to your TechniView account. The survey will be configured not to collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or identifying link details.

## How We Use the Information

We use this information to provide the coding practice service, show you your progress and execution results, operate and secure TechniView, troubleshoot problems, and evaluate usability and learning outcomes during the pilot. Pilot results may be used in research, presentations, or published reports.

## Who Can Access the Information

You can see your own progress and submission results. Authorized members of the TechniView pilot team may access identifiable information to operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and evaluate the platform. Other participants cannot see your individual results through TechniView.

TechniView is hosted on systems operated within the University of Cincinnati. Resend processes email used for account-related messages. UC Qualtrics hosts the pilot survey. Submitted code is run by the TechniView code-execution service.

## Research Data and Public Sharing

We plan to share both grouped summaries and a public, participant-level dataset. The public dataset will not include names, email addresses, submitted source code, or raw program output. It will include repeated pseudonymous codes for participants, problems, and assignment context, along with submission status, scores, error categories, late-work indicators, and relative timing information. Practice records will be labeled as practice and included alongside assignment records.

A repeated participant code lets records from the same participant be connected to each other. It will not display your name or email, but activity patterns may still make someone recognizable. At the end of the pilot, we will remove the link between participant codes and account identities before retaining or sharing the de-identified data. De-identified data may be kept indefinitely.

Submitted source code and raw execution output will not be included in the public dataset. At the end of the pilot, after creating the de-identified research dataset, we will delete the original submitted code and raw execution output.

## Storage and Retention

TechniView stores account, activity, submission, and execution information on systems operated within UC. Identifiable pilot data will be kept through the pilot. At the end of the pilot, we will create the de-identified research dataset and delete the original submitted code and raw execution output. The de-identified research dataset may be retained indefinitely.

## Your Choices and Questions

You may withdraw at any time by contacting Derek Corniello at corniedj@mail.uc.edu. Your pilot access will end and new activity collection will stop. We will retain survey responses already collected and data already included in grouped summaries. Other existing pilot data will be de-identified at the end of the pilot.

For questions about the pilot or this form, contact Derek Corniello at corniedj@mail.uc.edu.
