**Pilot Dates:** February 15 to March 14, 2027

## About the Pilot

TechniView is a coding practice platform. Several groups will use it during this four-week pilot. You can work at your own pace. There is no fixed number of hours you must spend.

During the pilot, you may complete assigned coding problems and explore other problems. We will use pilot results to evaluate usability and learning outcomes and may use them in research, presentations, or published reports.

Taking part is voluntary. You may decline or withdraw at any time without penalty. See “Your Choices and Questions” for what happens to data already collected.

---

## Information TechniView Collects

TechniView may collect and use the following information associated with your account and pilot activity:

- **Account information:** your name, email address, account role, account creation time, and email-verification status. TechniView uses an internal account ID to associate activity with your account. We do not collect a student ID for this pilot.
- **Problem activity:** the time you first open a problem, submission and completion times, attempts and retries, pass status, score, and error categories such as wrong answers, compilation errors, runtime errors, timeouts, and resource-limit errors.
- **Submitted work and execution results:** the source code you submit, including practice submissions; whether a run passes or fails; execution time and memory use; and program output, error output, or compiler output. TechniView stores submitted code and execution results.
- **Account and service security information:** account session records, email-verification and password-reset records, and source-address checks used to limit repeated signup, join, and account-recovery requests.
- **Survey answers:** a UC-hosted Qualtrics survey will ask about usability and your learning experience. Survey responses will not be linked to your TechniView account. The survey will be configured not to collect names, email addresses, or identifying link details.

---

## How We Use the Information

We use this information to provide the coding practice service, show you your progress and execution results, operate and secure TechniView, troubleshoot problems, and evaluate usability and learning outcomes during the pilot. Pilot results may be used in research, presentations, or published reports.

---

## Who Can Access the Information

You can see your own progress and submission results. Members of the TechniView pilot team who have completed the training required by the University of Cincinnati’s applicable IRB process may access identifiable information to operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and evaluate the platform. Other participants cannot see your individual results through TechniView.

TechniView is hosted on systems operated within the University of Cincinnati. Resend processes account-related email. UC-hosted Qualtrics hosts the pilot survey. The TechniView code-execution service runs submitted code.

---

## Research Data and Sharing

We plan to share grouped summaries and a de-identified research dataset. The dataset will not include names, email addresses, submitted source code, or raw program output. It will include repeated pseudonymous codes for participants, problems, and assignment context, along with submission status, scores, error categories, late-work indicators, and relative timing information.

Repeated participant codes let us connect records from the same person. The codes will not show your name or email, but activity patterns may still make someone recognizable. Before sharing the dataset, we will remove the link between participant codes and account identities.

---

## Storage and Retention

TechniView stores account, activity, submission, and execution information on systems operated within UC. At the end of the pilot, we will prepare the de-identified research dataset in accordance with applicable UC IRB requirements. Submitted code and raw program output will not be included in the shared dataset. The retention or deletion of those originals, and the retention period for other identifiable information, will follow applicable UC IRB requirements. The de-identified dataset may be kept indefinitely if those requirements allow.

---

## Your Choices and Questions

You may withdraw at any time by contacting Derek Corniello at corniedj@mail.uc.edu. Your pilot access will end, and TechniView will stop collecting new activity data about you. Data already collected will not be removed. We will retain survey responses and data already included in grouped summaries. Other existing pilot data will be de-identified at the end of the pilot, in accordance with applicable UC IRB requirements.

For questions about the pilot or this form, contact Derek Corniello at corniedj@mail.uc.edu.
