[[Block:Study information and consent]]
[[Question:DB]]
You are invited to take part in a research survey about TechniView, a coding-practice platform being developed by a University of Cincinnati student team. The survey asks instructors about possible uses of the platform in their courses and expected effects on student practice. You will see a short description and screenshots, then answer questions. It should take up to 10 minutes.

Taking part is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any time before submitting your responses. There is no payment or other compensation. Your decision will not affect your employment, teaching assignment, or relationship with the University of Cincinnati.

The survey will not ask for your name, email, department, or course name. The study team will configure the UC Qualtrics survey not to record IP address, geolocation, contact-list, or authenticator information. Please do not include names or other identifying details in your written response. The research team will limit access to authorized study personnel and retain study records according to UC requirements. Although the survey is designed not to collect direct identifiers, no online survey can guarantee that responses or technical records are impossible to identify.

Possible risks include discomfort answering questions about a teaching tool and a risk of loss of confidentiality. There may be no direct benefit to you. Your responses will be used to study instructors' views of TechniView, and the study team may use grouped findings in research presentations or publications. We do not plan to use or share the responses for a separate research study. For questions about the study or to request a summary of its results, contact Derek Corniello at corniedj@mail.uc.edu. Because the survey does not ask for contact information, we cannot send results to individual respondents automatically. For questions about your rights as a research participant, contact the UC Human Research Protection Program/IRB at (513) 558-5259 or irb@ucmail.uc.edu.

TechniView is a web-based coding practice platform. Students can work on programming problems and receive automated feedback on their submissions. Instructors can create courses and assignments and review student progress. The screenshots below show example screens. They use demonstration content and do not contain real student records.
